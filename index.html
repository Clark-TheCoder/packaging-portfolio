Emma Clark

Packaging Designer

ABOUT ME

I like packaging a bit more than the average person.

WHAT I DO: I am a packaging designer who translates brand identity into tactile experiences by aligning weight, materials, and finishes with a brand’s unique personality.

HOW I WORK: I discuss the brand and product with you, develop the visual direction, and bring the final concept to life using 3D software.

MY BACKGROUND: I hold a BBA in Marketing from Cape Breton University and a diploma in Computer Programming from Algonquin College. I work primarily in Blender and have a background in software development.

03 MAKEUP COMPACT

ARTICLES OF VIRTUE

GALLERY
ABOUT

BRAND DESCRIPTION

Luxury Makeup Brand

CONCEPT

Derived from the term "vertu", which describes small, well-crafted objects collected for their discerned value.
Articles of Virtue is a makeup brand that wants it's makeup compacts to be treasured long after use. The compact was designed to be a lasting item rather than a disposable beauty product.

LOGO

When repeated, the monogram forms a pattern reminiscent of traditional houndstooth. Referencing a textile long associated with tailored and refined fashion reinforces AOV's value of quality craftsmanship.

IDENTITY

Structural. Premium. Clean.

04 PRE WORKOUT POWDER

FLIGHT FUEL

GALLERY
ABOUT

BRAND DESCRIPTION

Performance Energy and Sports Nutrition Brand

CONCEPT

Designed like a jerrycan, the pre-workout container leans into the "fuel" theme tied to both the brand's name and the product itself. Because of this unique shape, rather than burying the scoop inside the powder like traditional brands, it snaps into an inset on top of the container.

IDENTITY

Industrial. Retro-futuristic. Novelty.
